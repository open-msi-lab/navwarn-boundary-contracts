- **NAVAREA IV warning texts** (`sources/navarea-iv-texts.jsonl` and the English

  Geospatial-Intelligence Agency and are works of the U.S. Government. They are
  given here as normalized for this study.
- **China Maritime Safety Administration warning texts are not included.** They are
  published by the China MSA at https://www.msa.gov.cn/ and are subject to its terms.
  `sources/source-index.csv` lists each sampled warning by identifier, year, warning
  number and the SHA-256 digest of the normalized text.
